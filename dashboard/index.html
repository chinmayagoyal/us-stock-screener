Method: filter, metrics and flags
Initial filter
Common stock only (no ETFs, funds, preferreds, warrants, units, SPACs) · market cap ≥ $2B · excludes Finance and Real Estate (banks, insurers and REITs need book-value or FFO models) · price ≥ $5 and 60-day median dollar volume ≥ $10M · at least 1 year of trading · SEC US-GAAP financials in USD no older than 200 days · trailing-12-month operating income and operating cash flow both positive · net debt/EBITDA ≤ 4× (or net cash) and EBIT/interest ≥ 3×.
Value score
EBIT/enterprise value, free-cash-flow yield, earnings yield (1/PE), forward earnings yield, shareholder yield (dividends + buybacks), sales/EV.
Quality score
Return on invested capital, gross profit/assets, Piotroski F-score (0–9), accruals (lower is better), net debt/EBITDA (lower is better), revenue growth.
Momentum, revisions, sentiment
12-1 and 6-1 month returns and price vs 200-day average; 90-day change in consensus EPS for this and next fiscal year, average EPS surprise and 30-day revision breadth; short interest (lower is better) and net insider buying.
Long-term score
60% value + 40% quality. Flag: LT score ≥ 90, value ≥ 70 and F-score ≥ 5.
Short-term score
35% momentum + 30% revisions + 20% value + 15% sentiment. Flag: ST score ≥ 90, value ≥ 40, positive 12-1 momentum and revisions ≥ 50.
Value-trap risk
Value ≥ 80 but quality < 30.
Implied FCF growth
The perpetual free-cash-flow growth the current price implies at a 9% cost of equity (a reverse DCF). Low or negative means the market expects little.
Data
Listings from Nasdaq; fundamentals rebuilt from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings; prices, estimates and short interest from Yahoo Finance (unofficial). XBRL tagging varies, so check any name against its filings before acting. Code and full workbook: github.com/chinmayagoyal/us-stock-screener.
